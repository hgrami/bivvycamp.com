The booking window, handled

Real campground data for Canada and the US. Bivvy computes the exact moment your reservation window opens, counts down to it, and reminds you the evening before. When it’s estimating, it says so. First-come sites get arrival advice and a plan B instead.

Meals that write the shopping list

Plan meals by day; their ingredients become the shopping list by themselves. Tick off what you have, note who’s bringing what, and buy the rest.

Packing without the panic

Gear per person or for the group, from a library that learns what you own. Everyone packs their own list — nothing left behind. Share one trip and the people on it tick their own things.

Yours, actually.

There is no Bivvy account and no Bivvy app backend. Everything lives in a database on your phone and works fully offline — in the backcountry included. Turn on iCloud sync and your own iCloud keeps your devices in step; it goes to Apple under your Apple ID, never to us. We cannot see it.

The free version is the real product, not a trial — and it stays free.
